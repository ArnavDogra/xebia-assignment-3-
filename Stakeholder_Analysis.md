### Stakeholder Pain Points
* **Placement Officer (Briefcase Icon):** Manual tracking, data silos.
* **Students (Graduation Cap Icon):** Anxiety, missing relevant jobs.
* **College Admin/Dean:** Needs high-level ROI and placement metrics.
* **Recruiting Companies (Building Icon):** Needs a quality talent pipeline quickly.
* **T&P Cell Staff:** Operational overload and coordination bottlenecks.
* **Parents (User Icon):** Needs assurance of successful placement.
